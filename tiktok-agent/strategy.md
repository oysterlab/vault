# Strategy Reference

## Account
- @oysterlab7 | Classic art slideshows (7 slides, 1024x1536px) | ~145 followers

## Scoring (0-100)
Tier 1 (60%): completion_rate×0.25 + share_rate×0.20 + save_rate×0.15
Tier 2 (30%): comment_rate×0.15 + slides_viewed×0.15
Tier 3 (10%): like_rate×0.10

Benchmarks: completion 70%=100, shares/views 1%=100, saves/views 2%=100, comments/views 0.5%=100, 7/7 slides=100, likes/views 5%=100

## Hook Formulas (ranked by performance)
1. **misconception_breaker** (30x avg views): "Everyone believes X. They are wrong."
   - Requires: iconic painting + universal misconception + one-sentence destruction
2. **hidden_detail** (avg 800 views): "Zoom into X. There is something no one notices."
3. **plot_twist** (avg 1,100 views): "The real meaning of X."
4. **personal_drama**: "The artist did X right after Y happened."

## Slide Structure
| # | Role | Key |

| 2 | Reveal | Shocking truth |
| 3 | Interactive | "Find it" element — increases dwell time |
| 4 | Tone shift | Humor/drama/chill moment |

| 6 | Bonus detail | Extra shock |
| 7 | CTA | Specific question |

## Rules
- All slide text in ENGLISH
- Only real famous paintings from Wikimedia Commons — NO AI-generated art
- 3 posts per cycle (A/B/C) — same painting, different visual approaches
- Visual variation = different crops + different slide sequences (not just text)
- No violent/gruesome art
- No duplicate paintings (check posts.json)
- Facts only — no exaggeration

## A/B/C Variation Checklist
- [ ] Slide 1 has different crop/composition for each post
- [ ] Detail slides zoom into different regions
- [ ] Slide sequence order is different (full→zoom vs zoom→full vs compare→detail)
